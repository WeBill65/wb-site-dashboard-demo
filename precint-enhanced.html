WeBill precinct explorer

A place for every connection.

Explore: choose The Nuvo Hotel, Block A Office, Block B Office, or The Commons Mall. Choose a level, space, then a meter. Search across the entire precinct by building name, room, suite, shop, utility, or meter ID.

Orbit: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, or right-drag / Shift-drag to pan. Pinch to zoom and use two fingers to pan on touch screens. The camera controls reset, zoom, show a plan, and switch between daylight and twilight.

Walk: drag to look, then use W A S D, arrow keys, or the on-screen pad to move. Hold Shift to move faster. Select levels in the directory to change floors. Escape returns to orbit. Click the minimap to move to a clear point; press Enter on the minimap to start at the current level's entrance.

Utilities: reveal schematic utility routes and filter water, electricity, alternative electricity, hot water, and gas. Select a meter and use Trace supply to follow its conceptual connection. X-ray reveals the building's level structure; individual levels open as furnished cutaways.

Concept model: the original precinct's hierarchy, shops, meter IDs and readings are preserved. Layouts, amenity heights, supply relationships and equipment positions are illustrative. No live account, tariff, allocation or operational control is connected.
