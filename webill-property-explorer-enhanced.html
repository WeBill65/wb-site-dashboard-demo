WeBill spatial explorer

Your property, from every angle.

Orbit: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, or right-drag / Shift-drag to pan. On a touch screen, pinch to zoom and use two fingers to pan.

Go inside: choose a residential floor, lobby or basement in the directory. Select a space, then a water or electricity meter. Meters in the model are also clickable.

Walk: drag to look around, then use W A S D, arrow keys, or the on-screen pad to move. Hold Shift to move faster. Use the floor buttons to change levels; this demo does not simulate lift travel. Escape returns to orbit.

See the services: enable Utilities to reveal illustrative supply routes. X-ray removes the facade to reveal the building's interiors. The utility checkboxes filter meter and route visibility. Click an asset's Trace supply button to inspect its connection.

This is a concept, not an as-built model. The property hierarchy, tenants, meter IDs, sample readings and tariffs come from the supplied demo. Geometry and routes are illustrative. No live connection, operational control or measured asset condition is implied.
