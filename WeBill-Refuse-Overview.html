The offering

Refuse removal, billed on what you actually collect

The same platform as water, electricity and gas.

WeBill is extending its utility-billing platform to refuse and waste management — a service tariff, not a meter reading. The billable quantity is the service the property actually receives: the receptacles, how often they are emptied, and the service level behind them. Cost-reflective charges, on the same account and audit trail that already run your utilities.

◆ Service-based ◆ Cost-reflective ◆ Re-rated on change ◆ Full provenance

From service point to posted account — one configurable, cost-reflective refuse engine.

What the offering covers

Six capabilities

Refuse service billing

Service entitlement × tariff — priced on what is collected, not on property value. Re-rated automatically whenever the service changes.

Service points & receptacles

The asset register behind every charge: bins, bulk containers and skips, each with size, quantity, lifts per week and effective dates.

Charging methods, configurable

Flat, receptacle × frequency, volume, weight, value-indexed or cost-of-service — set per municipality, category and zone with no code change.

Commercial & bulk waste

Skips by mass or volume, garden and building rubble as their own streams, and disposal priced at the landfill gate.

Free basic & indigent relief

The basic service free through the indigent register and above-basic charged in full, with revenue foregone reported separately from bad debt.

One consolidated account

Refuse, rates and utilities on a single monthly invoice, with a plain-language explanation of the service entitlement behind each charge line.

Compliance is built in, not bolted on

Grounded in the standards

Waste Act 59 of 2008

The service mandate and the service charges that fund it — determined under the Act.

Collection Standards

Weekly kerbside collection, with rigid, covered receptacles no bigger than 240 litres.

Pricing Strategy

Polluter-pays and full-cost recovery, with volumetric tariffs expressly contemplated.

mSCOA

Revenue classified as Service Charges → Waste Management, with VAT indicators.

POPIA

Owner, occupant and means-test data purpose-limited, access-controlled and access-logged.
