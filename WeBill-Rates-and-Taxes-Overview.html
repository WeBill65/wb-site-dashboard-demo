The offering

Municipal Rates &
Taxes, billed on
one platform

The same platform as water, electricity and gas.

WeBill is extending its utility-billing platform to property rates and taxes — a property tax calculated from a municipality's valuation roll and its cent-in-the-rand, then qualified by the relief in its Rates Policy. Rates are billed on the same account, the same meter-to-cash cycle and the same audit trail that already run your utilities, so one vendor carries the whole municipal revenue stack.

◆ One engine ◆ One account ◆ One audit trail ◆ Full provenance

From valuation roll to clearance certificate — one defensible, auditable rates engine.

What the offering covers

Six capabilities

Property rates billing

Rateable value × cent-in-the-rand, differentiated by property category and qualified by relief — calculated on one versioned engine, not a spreadsheet.

Valuation roll management

Import, version and reconcile general and supplementary rolls, with validity tracked against the statutory period and exceptions flagged before a roll goes live.

Relief & rebates

Exemptions, reductions, means-tested owner rebates and indigent relief — each with eligibility, expiry and re-verification, and revenue foregone reported separately from bad debt.

Section 118 clearance

Compute the two-year figure and issue clearance figures and certificates — the flagship workflow for conveyancers and managing agents on property transfers.

Objections & appeals

Valuation objections and billing queries run as cases with timers, document capture and an outcome recorded against the property — recalculating automatically when a value changes.

One consolidated account

Rates, refuse and utilities on a single monthly invoice, with a plain-language explanation behind every charge line and one audit trail across the lot.

Compliance is built in, not bolted on

Enforced in the engine

MPRA s19

Rate-ratio guardrail: every category randage is validated against the prescribed maximum before a resolution is published.

Roll validity

Tracked to the statutory period — four years for a metro, five for a local municipality — with alerts before expiry.

Provenance

Each line stores its roll, category and tariff version, so any charge can be reproduced and defended later.

mSCOA

Revenue classified to national reporting format, with VAT indicators, without re-keying.

POPIA

Owner and means-test data purpose-limited, access-controlled and access-logged.
